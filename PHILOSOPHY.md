# Philosophy

*Draft v2, from the owner interview of 2026-10-02. [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md) turns this page into
numbered rules. When the two disagree, this page wins and the rule is fixed.*

## What this is

One reader's study shelf. For each subject it holds the best lecture notes anyone has posted, converted
faithfully. Each set of notes is wrapped in a study layer written for that reader. The shelf is organised
by subject, so a question leads to the right chapter rather than to a pile of course years.

## The criterion it serves

From the knowledge base's `profile.md`:

> I can say that I understand something only when I can answer the question **how could I come up with
> this solution / problem / question / approach on my own?**

Everything we add exists to make that question answerable:

- **Motivation before formality.** State the problem that forces a definition before the definition.
- **Connections that reach outward.** Link to other courses, other fields, physics, optimisation,
  machine learning and computational biology. Staying inside the subject is not enough.
- **Proofs left to attempt.** A proof handed over removes the point, so every proof starts collapsed
  behind a hint ladder.

## Five tests every page must pass, in order

1. **Faithful.** The author's words and maths are theirs, unchanged, and keep their numbering. Nothing
   we wrote is presented as theirs.
2. **Traceable.** Every page, block, figure and number says where it came from. Anything computed can be
   recomputed from committed code.
3. **Teaches.** A chapter answers "why would anyone want this?" before "what is it?", and says where the
   idea leads.
4. **Placed.** Every chapter knows its prerequisites, its successor and its hub.
5. **Checked.** Every claim we add has been verified independently, by an agent that did not write it.

## What it refuses to be

- **A hoard.** One spine per course, never five course years stacked side by side. Other offerings feed
  the study layer; they are not concatenated.
- **A rewrite.** We add to the author's text and never paraphrase it. Where the author is unclear, the
  study layer explains; the core stays as written.
- **Decoration.** A figure must carry an argument. One exact plot beats three schematic sketches.
- **An answer key.** Hints come before solutions, and solutions come after attempts.

## Authorship decides where things live

| Where | Whose | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| library — core | the author's | the notes, converted faithfully |
| library — study layer | ours, written for the reader | roadmaps, motivation, figures, exercises, connections |
| knowledge base | the reader's own | questions, trajectories, derivations, logs, verdicts |

The library absorbs what the knowledge base used to call *adaptation*. The knowledge base keeps only
what its owner produces.

## What may be published

Material its author posted online may be published: course notes, slides, homework and solutions,
scribe notes, theses, and free book drafts. "Posted by the author" is the test. Being somewhere on the
internet is not enough, so the copy is always fetched from the author's own page, their course, their
institution, or arXiv. Commercially published books are never converted. A paper's full text appears
only under an open licence, though any paper may be summarised. Every page names its author and links to
the original. A rights-holder who asks for removal gets it, and the removal is recorded.

## When the rules run out

- **Cheap and reversible** (wording, figure choice, a chapter split, which exercises to use): take the
  documented default, log the decision with the rule it extends, and carry on.
- **Publication, deletion, restructuring, a tied spine choice or a hub change:** open a question, skip
  the item and wait for an answer.

A rule that keeps needing a decision is a missing rule. Add it to CONVENTIONS.md.
