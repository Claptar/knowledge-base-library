### **23.1 Patterns in Coin-Tossing**

We will say this in words. ( _Exercise_ : Rewrite the argument with mathematical notation for a general pattern.)

Fix the pattern _HTTHT_ . Toss a fair coin until we see this pattern: this requires _W_ tosses. _W_ is random and 5 _≤ W < ∞_ a.s. What is _EW_ ?

**Strategy 7** : Bet $1 that Toss 7 is _H_ . If we win the bet, bet $2 that Toss 8 is _T_ . If we win again, bet $4 that Toss 9 is _T_ . If we win again, bet $8 that Toss 10 is _H_ . If we win again, bet $16 that Toss 11 is _T_ .

**Overall Strategy** : Do strategy _i_ for each 1 _≤ i ≤ W_ and then stop after toss _W_ .

The OST tells us that _E_ [profit] = 0. The cost is _W_ and our return is 32 + 4 = 36 (because _HT_ is the start of the pattern). Therefore, _E_ [36 _− W_ ] = 0 so _EW_ = 36.

For a pattern of _HHHHH_ , we would have _EW_ = 32 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 = 62.

We can also show the existence of “non-transitive dice”: 3 patterns such that no matter what pattern you choose, I can choose a pattern such that the odds will be favorable that my pattern comes up before yours.
