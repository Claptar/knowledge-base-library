Pick a point _o ∈ M_ , a Riemannian manifold of dimension 2 or more, and with the help of the exponential map based at _o_ , define polar coordinates ( _r, θ_ ) and hence processes _r_ ( _Bt_ ) and _θ_ ( _Bt_ ) where _B_ is Brownian motion on _M_ . The _cutlocus of o_ , denoted _Co_ , is a subset of _M_ such that _r_ is smooth on _M −{o} − Co_ , the set _M − Co_ is a dense open subset of _M_ , and the distance from _o_ to _Co_ is positive.
