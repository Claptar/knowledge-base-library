The Bessel processes defined as the radial parts of Brownian motion in R<sup>_δ_</sup> are examples of _one-dimensional diffusions_ , that is to say strong Markov processes with continuous paths whose state space is a subinterval of R. Such processes have been extensively studied by a number of approaches: through their transition densities as solutions of a suitable parabolic differential equation, by space and time changes of Brownian motion, and as solutions of stochastic differential equations.
