It is natural in many contexts to consider Brownian motion and diffusions in some connected open subset _D_ of R<sup>_N_</sup> . The simplest such process is obtained _absorbing B_ when it first reaches the boundary at time _TD_ = inf _{t ≥_ 0 : _Bt ∈/ D}_ . This gives a Markov process _X_ , with state space the closure of _D_ , defined by _Xt_ = _Bt∧TD_ .
