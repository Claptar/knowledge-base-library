The BM in R<sup>_d_</sup> is transient for _d ≥_ 3, and the study of its rate of escape to _∞_ relies largely on the application of one-dimensional diffusion theory to the radial process. See for instance Shiga and Watanabe [387]. According to the theory of last exit decompositions, the _escape process_


is a process with independent increments, whose distribution can be described quite explicitly [150]. In the special case _d_ = 3 this process is the stable process
