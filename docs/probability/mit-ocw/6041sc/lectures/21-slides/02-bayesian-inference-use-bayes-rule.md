Types of Inference models/approaches

- Model building versus inferring unknown variables. E.g., assume _X_ = _aS_ + _W_

- Model building:

   - know “signal” _S_ , observe _X_ , infer _a_

- Estimation in the presence of noise: know _a_ , observe _X_ , estimate _S_ .

- Hypothesis testing: unknown takes one of few possible values; aim at small probability of incorrect decision

- Estimation: aim at a small estimation error
