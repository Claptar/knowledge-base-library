The engineers note the tire wear on front tires is often different than rear tires, and that the wear on the left side of the car is often different than the right side. To control for this variation, they propose a third design where each driver uses all four brands of tires, but the brand assigned to each tire position (frontleft, front-right, rear-left, rear-right) is randomized within each driver with care taken that each position is represented equally across brands.
