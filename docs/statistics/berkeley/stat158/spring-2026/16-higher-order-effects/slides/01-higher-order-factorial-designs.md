## Higher Order Factorial Design

Conceptually there is nothing that limits us to CR$$1$$ and CR$$2$$. Can do CR$$3$$, CR$$4$$, …

- In social sciences, higher order designs are less common &gt; small effect sizes and high measurement error means we need to use lots of data to get high power.
- In natural sciences, high order designs are more common &gt; larger effect sizes and low measurement error means we can detect effects with less data.
