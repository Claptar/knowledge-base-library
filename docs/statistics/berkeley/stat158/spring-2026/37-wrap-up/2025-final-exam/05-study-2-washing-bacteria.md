Two different washing solutions are compared to study their effectiveness in retarding bacteria growth. The washing solutions are applied to randomly selected five-gallon milk jugs and the response are the levels of bacteria remaining in the jugs after a half of a day. Lower values of the response indicated a more effective washing solution. Four milk jugs can be studied each day. Because ambient temperature is known to affect bacterial growth, the researchers decide to block on day to account for daily temperature differences.
