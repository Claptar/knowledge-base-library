# 7. Course Reading Guide

## What this covers

This chapter is not taught material — it is the course's own reading plan, reproduced so that a
reader working through this book knows which textbook chapter each part of the course was assigned
against. Berkeley STAT 156/256 (Causal Inference), Fall 2024, was built on two textbooks, read in
parallel and interleaved lecture by lecture. Assumes nothing beyond wanting a map from lecture to
chapter.

## The texts

- Peng Ding, *A First Course in Causal Inference*. A preprint is freely available on arXiv:
  <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.18793>.
- James M. Robins and Miguel A. Hernán, *Causal Inference: What If*. Available online at
  <https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/>.

Most lectures were assigned a chapter of Ding alone; several paired it with the corresponding
section or chapter of Robins and Hernán.
