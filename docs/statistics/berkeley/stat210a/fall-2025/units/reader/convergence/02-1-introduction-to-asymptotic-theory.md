# 1 Introduction to Asymptotic Theory

**Source:** [`units/reader/convergence.html`](https://github.com/berkeley-stat210a/fall-2025/blob/5eb849a4924c34eb73e098cdfc812ffa8d806501/units/reader/convergence.html) · **Licence:** CC BY 4.0 · Converted 2026-09-14 from `.html` (good)

So far, everything has been finite sample, often using special properties of model <span class="math inline">\$\\cP\$</span> (e.g., exponential family) to do exact calculations. For generic models, exact calculations may be intractable or impossible. However, we may be able to approximate our problem with a simpler problem in which calculations are easy.

Typically, we approximate by Gaussian by taking the limit as observations <span class="math inline">\$n \\to \\infty\$</span>. But this is only interesting if the approximation is good for reasonable sample sizes.
