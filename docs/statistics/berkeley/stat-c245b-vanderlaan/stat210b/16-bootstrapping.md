Densities can be very poorly behaved and hard to estimate. The easiest way out is to just use the bootstrap, which provides automatic confidence intervals. The bootstrap has been shown to work for the median, and under very general conditions for asymptotically linear estimators. In fact, the sample median was one of the examples discussed in Efron’s 1979 paper where he invented the bootstrap method.
