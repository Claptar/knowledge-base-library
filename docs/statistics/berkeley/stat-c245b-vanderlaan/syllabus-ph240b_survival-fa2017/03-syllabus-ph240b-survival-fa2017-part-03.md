## **Materials**

The weekly updated schedule, lecture videos, scribed notes, R lab assignments, homework assignments, supplemental materials, and blog information will be provided on bCourses.

The following textbooks serve as excellent references, encompassing all of the topics covered in this course:

- _Targeted Learning: Causal Inference for Observational and Experimental Data_ by Mark van der Laan and Sherri Rose (2011)

- _The Statistical Analysis of Failure Time Data, Second Edition_ by John Kalbfleisch and Ross Prentice (2002)

## **Prerequisites**

STAT 201A-B (may be taken concurrently) or older version STAT 200A-B or consent of instructor. PB HLTH C240A / STAT C245A is recommended. Familiarity with the R language.
