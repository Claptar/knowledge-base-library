|**Week**|**Content**|
|---|---|
|1: Aug. 23|_• Topics:_ Course introduction.|
|2: Aug. 28 – 30|_• Topics:_ Recap of fundamentals for single experiment: data, model, target pa-<br>rameter, efficient influence curve, exact second order remainder, least favorable<br>sub-model, loss function, TMLE, analysis of TMLE.<br>_• Reading:_ Chapters 4 – 5 vdL&R (2011) book.|
|3: Sept. 04 – 06|_• Topics:_ Sequential adaptive design, likelihood of sequential adaptive design,<br>statistical model, statistical estimation problem, oracle fixed design, adaptive<br>design converging to oracle fixed design, examples of oracle fixed design.<br>_• Reading:_ Section 1 vdL (2008) tech report.|
|4: Sept. 11 – 13|_• Topics:_ Sequential adaptive design learning optimal individualized treatment<br>rule, multiple bandit problem, Sequential adaptive design maximizing infor-<br>mation for answering question of interest.<br>_• Reading:_ Chapter 29 vdL&R (2011) book, Chapter 24 vdL&R (2018) book,<br>Section 2 vdL (2008) tech report.|
|5: Sept. 18 – 20|_• Topics:_ MLE for adaptive design likelihood assuming parametric model, anal-<br>ysis of MLE, martingale CLT, asymptotic equicontinuity of martingale process,<br>MLE treating parametric model as working model, analysis of this MLE, para-<br>metric versus realistic models for adaptive designs.<br>_• Reading:_ Chapter 29 vdL&R (2011) book, Sections 4–5 vdL (2008) tech report.|
|6: Sept. 25 – 27|_• Topics:_ TMLE of target parameter for sequential adaptive design, general.<br>_• Reading:_ vdL (2008) tech report, Chapter 24 vdL&R (2018) book.<br>_•_ **Project Presentations I – Proposal on Tues Sept. 25**.|
|7: Oct. 02 – 04|_• Topics:_ TMLE of ATE for sequential adaptive design, analysis of TMLE.<br>_• Reading:_ Chambaz & vdL (2014) SJoS article, vdL (2008) tech report, Chapter<br>29 vdL&R (2011) book.|
|8: Oct. 09 – 11|_• Topics:_ TMLE of mean outcome under optimal rule for sequential adaptive<br>design, analysis of TMLE.<br>_• Reading:_ Chapter 24 vdL&R (2018), Chapter 29 vdL&R (2011), Chambaz,<br>Zheng & vdL (2017) AoS article.|
|9: Oct. 16 – 18|_• Topics:_ Highly Adaptive Lasso (HAL) for sequential adaptive designs and<br>more general time-series (i.e. ordered sequence of experiments with common<br>parameters), analysis of HAL.<br>_• Reading:_ TBA|
|10: Oct. 23 – 25|_• Topics:_ Online super-learning for sequential adaptive designs and more gen-<br>eral time-series, oracle inequality for online cross-validation selector.<br>Ivana<br>Malenica presents online super-learner R package.<br>_• Reading:_ Chapter 18 vdL&R (2018) book, Benkeser et al. (2017) SiM article.|
