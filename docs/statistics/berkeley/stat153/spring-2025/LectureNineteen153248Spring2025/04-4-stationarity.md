**Definition 4.1** (Stationarity) **.** _A doubly infinite sequence of random variables yt is stationary_

_1. The mean of yt (denoted by_ E _yt) is the same for all times t_

_2. The covariance between yt_ 1 _and yt_ 2 _only depends on the distance |t_ 1 _− t_ 2 _| between t_ 1 _and t_ 2 _._

The third example of the previous section is a stationary model while the other two models are not stationary because the means therein change with time. We shall see more examples of stationary models next week.
