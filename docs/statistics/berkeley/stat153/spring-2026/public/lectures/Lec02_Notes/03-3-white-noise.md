Many real-world time series are a combination of underlying signal _st_ plus noise _wt_ . In some (nice) cases, _wt_ is _white noise_ .

White noise is a special case of uncorrelated variables in sequence, e.g. _xt_ where _t_ = 1 _,_ 2 _,_ 3 _, ..._ . White noise, unlike most real time series, _is_ iid, with mean 0 and variance _σw_<sup>2.OneveryusefulcaseiswhitenoisefromaGaussian</sup> distribution, where we can write: _wt ∼_ iid _N_ (0 _, σw_<sup>2).</sup>

If all time series could be described in this way, classical statistics would suffice.
