# Take care of yourself {#take-care-of-yourself .unnumbered}

**Source:** [`syllabus/syllabus.tex`](https://github.com/berkeley-stat153/fall-2024/blob/94c943d315ea7361f1f660f42881d219a7e7f009/syllabus/syllabus.tex) · **Licence:** CC BY 4.0 · Converted 2026-09-14 from `.tex` (high)

Take care of yourself. Do your best to maintain a healthy lifestyle this semester by eating well, exercising, getting enough sleep, and taking some time to relax. This will probably help you achieve your goals and cope with stress.

All of us benefit from support during times of struggle. You are not alone. There are many helpful resources available on campus. You can find these linked from the Academic Accomodations Hub <https://evcp.berkeley.edu/programs-resources/academic-accommodations-hub>.
