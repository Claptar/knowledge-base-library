# $MA(1)$ vs $AR(1)$

$$y_t = \mu + \varepsilon_t + \theta \varepsilon_{t-1}$$
**ALWAYS STATIONARY**

$$y_t - \phi_1 y_{t-1} = \phi_0 + \varepsilon_t$$
**NOT NECESSARILY CAUSAL STATIONARY**

---

**Parameter Estimation:**

$\phi_0, \phi_1, \sigma$
$y = (y_t)$
$X = [1, y_{t-1}]$
`sm.OLS(y, X).fit()`

**Likelihood:**

$(y_1, \dots, y_n) \sim N\left(\begin{pmatrix} \mu \\ \vdots \\ \mu \end{pmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} \ddots \end{bmatrix}\right)$

$$\operatorname{var}(y_t) = \operatorname{var}(\mu + \varepsilon_t + \theta \varepsilon_{t-1}) = \sigma^2 (1 + \theta^2)$$

$$\operatorname{Cov}(y_t, y_{t+1}) = \theta \sigma^2$$

$$\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma^2(1+\theta^2) & \theta \sigma^2 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ \theta \sigma^2 & \sigma^2(1+\theta^2) & \theta \sigma^2 & 0 \dots & 0 \\ 0 & \theta \sigma^2 & \sigma^2(1+\theta^2) & \theta \sigma^2 0 \dots 0 \\ & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & \\ 0 & \dots & 0 & \theta \sigma^2 & \sigma^2(1+\theta^2) \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\frac{1}{(\sqrt{2\pi})^n \sqrt{\det \Sigma}} \exp\left[-\frac{1}{2} (y - \mu)^T \Sigma^{-1} (y - \mu)\right]$$

$$y = \begin{pmatrix} y_1 \\ \vdots \\ y_n \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mu = \begin{pmatrix} \mu \\ \vdots \\ \mu \end{pmatrix}$$

**ARIMA** $\to$ function in `statsmodels`

---

**AR model:** $y_t - \phi_1 y_{t-1} - \dots - \phi_p y_{t-p} = \phi_0 + \varepsilon_t$

**MA:** $y_t = \mu + \varepsilon_t + \theta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + \theta_2 \varepsilon_{t-2} + \dots + \theta_q \varepsilon_{t-q}$

**ARMA($p, q$):**

$$y_t - \phi_1 y_{t-1} - \dots - \phi_p y_{t-p} = \phi_0 + \varepsilon_t + \theta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + \dots + \theta_q \varepsilon_{t-q}$$

**ARIMA($p, d, q$):**
$\downarrow$ differencing
Take data $y_t$, difference $d$ times & then use $ARMA(p, q)$

`arima(data, order = (p, d, q))`

$MA(q)$: `arima(data, order = (0, 0, q))`

$MA(q)$ to $y_t - y_{t-1}$:
- `arima(differenced data, order = (0, 0, q))`
- `arima(data, order = (0, 1, q))`

$AR(p)$: `arima(data, order = (p, 0, 0))`

## **$AR(p)$** AutoReg

$$\phi(B) y_t = \phi_0 + \varepsilon_t$$

$$\phi(z) = 1 - \phi_1 z - \phi_2 z^2 - \dots - \phi_p z^p \to AR(p) \text{ polynomial}$$

Roots of $\phi(z)$:
Moduli of every root $> 1 \to$ Causal Stationary Regime

$$y_t = \mu + \varepsilon_t + \psi_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + \psi_2 \varepsilon_{t-2} + \dots$$

**$AR(1)$:** $|\phi_1| < 1$

$$y_t = \frac{\phi_0}{1 - \phi_1} + \varepsilon_t + \phi_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + \phi_1^2 \varepsilon_{t-2} + \phi_1^3 \varepsilon_{t-3} + \dots$$
