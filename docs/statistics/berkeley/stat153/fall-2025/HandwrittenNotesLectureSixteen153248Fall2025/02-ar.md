# AR($p$)

$$y_t = \phi_0 + \phi_1 y_{t-1} + \dots + \phi_p y_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t$$
$$t = p+1, \dots, n$$

$$
y = \begin{pmatrix} y_{p+1} \\ \vdots \\ y_n \end{pmatrix} \quad X = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & y_p & y_{p-1} & \dots & y_1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & y_{n-1} & y_{n-2} & \dots & y_{n-p} \end{bmatrix}
$$

---

Unknown parameters: $\phi_0, \phi_1, \dots, \phi_p \quad \sigma$

**Estimation & Inference:**

$y, X \qquad$ `sm.OLS(y, X).fit()`

$\hat{\phi}_0, \hat{\phi}_1, \dots, \hat{\phi}_p$ : Least Squares

$$\min_{\phi_0, \dots, \phi_p} \sum_{t=p+1}^n (y_t - \phi_0 - \phi_1 y_{t-1} - \dots - \phi_p y_{t-p})^2$$

$$\hat{\sigma}_{\text{MLE}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-p} \sum_{t=p+1}^n (y_t - \hat{\phi}_0 - \dots - \hat{\phi}_p y_{t-p})^2}$$

**Forecasting:** $y_{n+1}, y_{n+2}, \dots, y_{n+k}$

$$\hat{y}_{n+1} = \hat{\phi}_0 + \hat{\phi}_1 y_n + \hat{\phi}_2 y_{n-1} + \dots + \hat{\phi}_p y_{n+1-p}$$
$$\hat{y}_{n+2} = \hat{\phi}_0 + \hat{\phi}_1 \hat{y}_{n+1} + \hat{\phi}_2 y_n + \dots + \hat{\phi}_p y_{n+2-p}$$
$$\boxed{\hat{y}_{n+i} = \hat{\phi}_0 + \hat{\phi}_1 \hat{y}_{n+i-1} + \dots + \hat{\phi}_p \hat{y}_{n+i-p}}$$
$$\boxed{\hat{y}_j = y_j \quad \text{if } j \le n} \qquad i = 1, 2, \dots$$
