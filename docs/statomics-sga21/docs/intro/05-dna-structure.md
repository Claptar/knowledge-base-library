# DNA structure

Polynucleotide chains are directional molecules with slightly different ends: 3’ end and 5’ end. 3’ and 5’ refers to carbon atom numbering in the sugar ring. (3’ hydroxyl group, 5’ phosphate group) Complementary DNA strands are antiparallel (i.e, 5’ to 3’ ends for each strand are opposite) Most of it is coiled and condensed: very stable
