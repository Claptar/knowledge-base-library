1. There are n fish in a lake, some of which are green and the rest blue. Each day, Helen catches 1 fish. She is equally likely to catch any one of the n fish in the lake. She throws back all the fish, but paints each green fish blue before throwing it back in. Let Gi denote the event that there are i green fish left in the lake.

   - (a) Show how to model this fishing exercise as a Markov chain, where {Gi} are the states. Explain why your model satisfies the Markov property.

   - (b) Find the transition probabilities {pij}.

   - (c) List the transient and the recurrent states.
