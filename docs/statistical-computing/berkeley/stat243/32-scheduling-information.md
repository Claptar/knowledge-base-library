# 32. Scheduling information

## What this covers

STAT 243 is a statistical computing course, and this chapter collects the administrative shape of
the term rather than any statistical or computational content: how the two in-class assessments,
the term project, and the opening weeks' labs and self-study assignments are organised. The course
has run at least three times — Fall 2024, Fall 2025, and Fall 2026 — and the schedule page for each
offering is drawn on here, since the assessment format and the required early work both changed
from year to year. This assumes only that the reader is following (or considering) the course and
wants to know how the term's work is timed, not any prior background.

## In-class assessments

Every offering places two closed, in-person assessments during the term — one in October, one
close to the end of November — each preceded by a review session held a few days earlier in
section. What differs across years is the name given to the assessment and, in Fall 2026, whether
it is split by course number:

| Offering | Name | Assessment 1 | Review 1 | Assessment 2 | Review 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fall 2024 | Quiz | Wed Oct 23, in class | Fri Oct 18 | Mon Nov 25, in class | Fri Nov 22 |
| Fall 2025 | Mini-exam | Oct 22, in class | Oct 17 | Nov 24, in class | Nov 21 |
| Fall 2026 | Exam (243A / 243B) | Mon Oct 12, in class | Oct 9 | Mon Nov 23, in class | Nov 20 |

The Fall 2024 schedule additionally splits Quiz 1 by room and last name: students with last names
A–T sit it in Evans 60, and V–Z in Evans 340.

## The term project

Fall 2024 and Fall 2025 both carry a term project, with the due date given only as "TBD during exam
week" at the time the schedule page was written. The Fall 2026 schedule, as given here, does not
mention a project at all.

## Labs and self-study before the term is under way

All three offerings share the same shape for the first two weeks:

- **An optional command-line help session** — "Lab 0" in 2024 and 2025, "Lab A0" in 2026 — held the
  week before classes start (August 30 in 2024 and 2025, August 28 in 2026). Unlike every later lab
  it is walk-in: students can arrive at any time to get software installed, get access to a
  UNIX-style command line, and work through a "UNIX basics" tutorial. The expectation is that this
  is done, and command-line basics are at least underway, before the first class meeting of the
  following week.
- **The first mandatory, section-specific lab** — "Lab 1" in 2024 and 2025, "Lab A1" in 2026 — on
  Git, setting up a GitHub repository for problem sets, and Quarto for dynamic documents. Held
  September 6 (2024), September 5 (2025), September 4 (2026). Students are asked to attend only the
  section they are registered for, given room capacity, unless they have arranged otherwise with
  the instructor.
- **An optional library LaTeX session**, recommended in each year's schedule "in particular if you
  are a Statistics graduate student": even a student who will write problem sets in Quarto, Jupyter
  notebooks, or R Markdown rather than LaTeX directly still needs LaTeX math syntax, since that is
  the syntax those tools render.

Fall 2024 and Fall 2025 additionally set two pieces of self-study, graded only as complete or not,
submitted through Gradescope, with no requirement on formatting, commentary, or even whether the
answers are typed or handwritten:

- **A bash shell tutorial**, to be worked through by September 9 (2024) or September 8 (2025),
  skipping the tutorial's pages on regular expressions and on managing processes. The first ten
  problems from the tutorial's accompanying exercises are submitted separately.
- **Fall 2024 only, a follow-up on regular expressions**: reading the regex page of the same
  tutorial and submitting answers to a separate set of regex practice problems by September 13.

Neither of these two assignments appears in the Fall 2026 schedule as given here.
