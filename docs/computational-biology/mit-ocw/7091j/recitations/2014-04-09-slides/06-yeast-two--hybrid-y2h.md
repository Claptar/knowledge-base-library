# Yeast Two-­‐Hybrid (Y2H)

- -­‐ Used to detect interactors with your bait protein of interest

- -­‐ Introduce two plasmids into yeast cells:

   - -­‐ 1. DNA-­‐binding   domain (DBD) -­‐ Bait fusion (in this case, **Snf1** is bait)

   - -­‐ 2. Prey -­‐ AcPvator domain (AD) fusion (in this case **Snf4** is prey)

      - -­‐ AD needed to recruit PolII for transcripPon of reporter gene -­‐ O@en will screen a library of potenPal prey molecules

   - -­‐ This example uses the well characterized Gal4 transcripPon acPvator protein in yeast.

   - -­‐ _lacZ_ transcripPon can be detected by colorimetric inspecPon (can use other reporter genes such at metabolic enzyme (His producPon) and growth on minimal media lacking His)

- -­‐ Y2H will miss interacPons for prey proteins that are not soluble and/or don’t   localize to the

- nucleus

- -­‐ But can detect more transient interacPons that may not be captured by affinity purificaPon
