## Minimal biology review

- DNA is composed of 4 nucleotides: A, C, G, T

- DNA is _transcribed_ into mRNA which is _translated_ into protein

- A _gene_ is a said to be _expressed_ when it is transcribed

- _Transcription factors (TF)_ are proteins that bind DNA and affect (promote/repress) gene expression

- A _DNA sequence motif_ can be a sequence where specific TFs bind (others too – eg. splicing signals for mRNA)
