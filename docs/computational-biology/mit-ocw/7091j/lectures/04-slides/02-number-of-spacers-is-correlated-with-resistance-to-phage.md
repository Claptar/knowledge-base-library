# Number of spacers is correlated with resistance to phage

**Fig. 6.** Correlation of *S. thermophilus* phage resistance and the number of spacers in a CRISPR locus. Filled symbols correspond to data obtained from strains tested with the panel of 59 phages. The line of best-fit refers to strains that were not fully phage resistant ($\bullet$), and for which $y = -0 \cdot 02x + 0 \cdot 77$ and $R^2 = 0 \cdot 51$. Fully phage-resistant strains ($\blacksquare$), were not taken into account for the correlation shown. $\bigcirc$, Strains tested with the panel of seven phages.
