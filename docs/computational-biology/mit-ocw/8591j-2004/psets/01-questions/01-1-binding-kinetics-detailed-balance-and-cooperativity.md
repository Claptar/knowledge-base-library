- (10) _a._ In the figure, the _Ki_ represent association constants in inverse concentration units. According to detailed balance, the individual binding reactions are in equilibrium. Find two different expressions for the equilibrium ratio [ _DXAXB_ ]/[ _D_ ][ _X_ ]<sup>2</sup> , corresponding to the two possible binding paths. Can all four association constants be independently specified?

- (10) _b._ The total DNA concentration is [ _Dtot_ ] = [ _D_ ]+[ _DXA_ ]+[ _DXB_ ]+[ _DXAXB_ ]. If _K2_ >> _K1_ and _K4_ >> _K3_ , show that the singly-bound forms [ _DXA_ ] and [ _DXB_ ] form a negligible fraction of this total. This corresponds to a cooperative system in which the second binding is highly encouraged once the first has already occurred.

- (10) _c._ Assuming [ _Dtot_ ] ≈ [ _D_ ]+[ _DXAXB_ ], find the bound fraction _f =_ [ _DXAXB_ ]/[ _Dtot_ ] in terms of [ _X_ ] and the association constants.
