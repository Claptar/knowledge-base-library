> autophosphorylation: `CheA` + `ATP` ↔ `CheA` + `ADP p`

phosphoryltransfer:

`CheA` + `CheY` ↔ `CheA` + `CheY p p`

CheYp binds to the motor (FliM), motor rotates CW (= tumbles)

_logic:_

high levels of CheA -> high levels of CheYp

(lots of tumbles)

low levels of CheA -> low levels of CheYp

(straight swimming)
