**Phosphorylation (CheA, CheY, CheB) CheA** (protein kinase), uses ATP to phosphorylate one of its histidines. `CheA` + `ATP` ↔ `CheA` + `ADP p` **CheA (CheAp)** is bound to the Tar receptor through an adapter protein **CheW** . **CheW** is not known to have any enzymatic activity. (these proteins are sometimes called ‘scaffolding protein’)
