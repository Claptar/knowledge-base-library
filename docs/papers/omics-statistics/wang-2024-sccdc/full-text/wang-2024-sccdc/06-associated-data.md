## Data Availability Statement {#_adda93_}

The scCDC R package is available at  [59]. The processed datasets and the code scripts used to generate the figures are available on Zenodo (10.5281/zenodo.6905189) [60].

The published single-cell and single-nuclei datasets were downloaded from the GEO, Synapse databases, 10 × Genomics, and Human Cell Atlas with the accession numbers listed in Additional file 2: Table S1 (syn21694522 [61], [GSM4878207](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4878207) [62], CRA007450 [63], [GSM5073381](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM5073381) [64], [GSE151048](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE151048) [65], [GSM4230078](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4230078) [66], SRR10751504 [67], [GSM2177570](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2177570) [68], [GSM3489185](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM3489185) [69], [GSM3587009](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM3587009) [70], [GSM5024089](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM5024089) [71], FCAImmP7528289 [72], [GSE142653](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE142653) [73],  [74],  [75]). The single-nuclei RNA-seq data of mouse mammary glands are deposited in the Genome Sequence Archive at the National Genomics Data Center, China National Center for Bioinformation / Beijing Institute of Genomics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (GSA: CRA007450) [63].

The total RNA-seq data in mouse lactating and virgin mammary glands are from the GEO database with accession numbers: [GSE115370](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE115370) [76, 77] and [GSE52016](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE52016) [78, 79], respectively. The total RNA-seq data from mouse pancreatic islets were downloaded from GEO: [GSE148809](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE148809) [80, 81]. The genes encoding secretory proteins were predicted in SignalP 4.0 [82], and the list of protein-coding genes were obtained from the Refseq database [83].
